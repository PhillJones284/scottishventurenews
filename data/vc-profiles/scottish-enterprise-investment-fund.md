SEIF co-invests alongside private capital across virtually every stage and sector and does not lead rounds or set terms — its presence signals that a lead investor has already committed, not that public capital is driving the deal. For founders, SEIF involvement is close to a given for any credible deal involving Scottish angel syndicates or institutional co-investors. No independent thesis can be read from its deal selection given its breadth.
